Chuyang Wei

Ph.D. Student in Computer Science and Technology

I’m a Ph.D. student at the University of Science and Technology of China, jointly trained at Beijing Zhongguancun Academy.

I study self-improving agents: learning from delayed feedback, acquiring reusable skills, and transferring experience from agent harnesses into model weights. My work uses open-world forecasting as a testbed.
