Chuyang Wei

Ph.D. Student in Computer Science · USTC × Beijing Zhongguancun Academy

I study self-improving LLM agents: learning from delayed feedback, evolving persistent agent harnesses, and internalizing reusable experience into model weights, using open-world forecasting as a testbed.

Currently focusing on Recursive Self-Improvement (RSI) and Benchmark RSI — uncovering and formalizing genuine, hard problems where self-improvement truly matters.
